Welcome to Zoodles: Little Learners – a magical, interactive, and completely safe playground designed for toddlers, preschoolers, and young children to play, learn, and grow!

Built with love and designed for independent learning, Zoodles offers a collection of educational games, creative coloring books, memory match puzzles, and voice-narrated stories that keep young minds engaged without any distractions.

Why Parents and Kids Love Zoodles:
• 100% Offline Play: Perfect for road trips, flights, and remote areas. No internet connection or mobile data required.

Learn the alphabet from A to Z with voice guidance, cute animals, and fun interactive letter cards.
2. Counting & Math Games
Master counting, basic addition, and simple subtraction using colourful interactive cards and objects.
3. Interactive Stories
Engaging, narrated short stories and nursery rhymes that foster reading comprehension and phonics.
4. Memory Matching Game
Train memory, recognition, and concentration skills with customizable card matching games in multiple difficulty levels.
5. Coloring Book
Unleash creativity! Color adorable animals, fruits, and shapes using a rich, kid-friendly palette.
6. Jigsaw Puzzles
Develop motor skills and spatial reasoning by assembling simple and fun puzzles with adjustable grid sizes.

We are fully committed to your child's privacy. Zoodles operates completely offline. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data, location info, or device identifiers. It is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.
