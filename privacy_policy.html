Privacy Policy for Zoodles: Little Learners

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Techbitss ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Zoodles: Little Learners mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of children and all users who use our App.

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection, use, and disclosure in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

1. 100% Offline Operation & No Data Collection

Zoodles: Little Learners is designed to operate completely offline.

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect, request, or store any personal information (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical locations).
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect persistent device identifiers, IP addresses, or advertising IDs.
  • Local Storage Only: The App saves game progress locally on the user's device (e.g., completed cards, earned badges). This data is stored locally using the device's secure storage and is never transmitted to any external server.

2. No Third-Party Ads or Analytics

To maintain a safe, kid-friendly environment:

  • We do not display third-party advertisements.
  • We do not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics tracking tools, or social media plugins.

3. No Internet or Sensitive Permissions

The App does not request access to the internet, camera, microphone, contacts, physical location, or external device storage.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
